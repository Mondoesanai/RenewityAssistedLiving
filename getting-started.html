Getting Started

Moving a parent into care is hard. Getting started here shouldn't be.

No sales scripts, no pressure. Just a clear path from your first phone call to move-in day.

01

Reach out & talk it through

Call or send us a message. We'll ask about your loved one's needs, routines, and personality — and answer your questions honestly, including on cost.

02

Tour the home

Walk through Renewity in person, meet the caregivers, and see exactly what a typical day looks like. Bring your loved one along if they're able.

03

Share health & care information

We'll ask for a basic health history and physician information so we can build a personalized care plan — nothing more invasive than needed.

04

Plan the paperwork & move-in date

We'll walk you through agreements and logistics together, and set a move-in date that works for your family's timeline.

05

Welcome home

We help your loved one settle in with familiar belongings and get introduced to their new routine, neighbors, and caregivers.
