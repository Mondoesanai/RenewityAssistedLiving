Our Mission

Renewing life. Redefining care.

Renewity was founded on one conviction: aging loved ones deserve to be nurtured and cherished, not managed. As the years unfold, health can wane and roles within a family can shift — but every day still holds new hope. That transition away from full independence shouldn't mean losing dignity, choice, or comfort.

We offer a sanctuary of safety, warmth, and love for seniors who need help with daily activities — from dining and bathing to staying mobile — where compassion and respect run through every interaction. Renewity is where you'll find not just excellent care, but a true sense of belonging.

Why Renewity?

A home-style ratio, not a facility-sized one.

Renewity maintains a resident-to-caregiver ratio of about 5:1 — as few as four residents per caregiver at any time. Compare that to the roughly 20:1 ratio common in larger, facility-based communities. That smaller number is the whole difference: personalized attention, stronger relationships, and caregivers who actually know your preferences and routines.

Personalized Attention

A low ratio means caregivers have real time with each resident — not a checklist rushed between rooms.

Daily Social & Cognitive Support

A diverse mix of daily activities builds social, emotional, and cognitive engagement, day after day.

Transparent Family Communication

We keep families informed about health and well-being — proactively, not just when something changes.

A Day in the Life

Dining, activities, and family — every day.

Dining

Delicious, home-cooked meals served in our bright, open dining room — prepared fresh in our full-size kitchen around each resident's preferences and health needs. Caregivers are attentive around the clock with refreshments and snacks.

Activities

Renewity features the Tovertafel®, a leading interactive gaming system for complex and differentiated care needs — plus regular hairdresser and nail visits, chair exercise, movies, music, crafts, and holiday celebrations, all tailored to our small community.

Family Access

As a resident, Renewity is your home — so family is encouraged to visit often. We make accommodations for family to share meals and join daily activities, all part of making everyone feel comfortable and welcome.
