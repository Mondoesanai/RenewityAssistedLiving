Our Mission

Renewing life. Redefining care.

Renewity was founded on one conviction: aging loved ones deserve to be nurtured and cherished, not managed. As the years unfold, health can wane and roles within a family can shift — but every day still holds new hope. That transition away from full independence shouldn't mean losing dignity, choice, or comfort.

We offer a sanctuary of safety, warmth, and love for seniors who need a hand with daily living — from dining and bathing to staying mobile — where compassion and respect run through every interaction. Renewity is where you'll find not just excellent care, but a true sense of belonging.

Why Renewity?

A home-style ratio, not a facility-sized one.

Renewity maintains a resident-to-caregiver ratio of about 5:1. Compare that to the roughly 20:1 ratio common in larger, facility-based communities. That smaller number is the whole difference: personalized attention, stronger relationships, and caregivers who actually know your preferences and routines.

Personalized Attention

A low ratio means caregivers have real time with each resident — not a checklist rushed between rooms.

Daily Social & Cognitive Support

A diverse mix of daily activities builds social, emotional, and cognitive engagement, day after day.

Transparent Family Communication

We keep families informed about health and well-being — proactively, not just when something changes.

Our Care Model

A congregate care home — clearly defined.

We believe families make better decisions with a clear picture of what we do — and just as importantly, what we don't.

What We Provide

  • Help with cooking and home-cooked meals
  • Help with cleaning and housekeeping
  • Help with bathing, dressing, and hygiene
  • Help staying mobile and safe around the home
  • Medication reminders
  • Awake, present caregivers overnight
  • Daily social, emotional & cognitive activities

What We Are Not

  • Not a skilled nursing facility
  • Not a hospital or medical treatment center
  • No clinical staff providing medical treatment
  • No rehabilitation or physical therapy services
  • No IV therapy, wound care, or skilled nursing procedures
