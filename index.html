What We're Not

This isn't a nursing home.
It's a residential care home.

Renewity is a residential care home for seniors — licensed to support daily living, not a skilled nursing or rehabilitation facility. That distinction matters. It means help with dressing, bathing, meals, and medication reminders, delivered in an actual house on a real street, by caregivers who live in the community, not a clinical unit run on shift changes.
