Life with Renewity

Connection is care.

At Renewity, we create daily opportunities for residents to engage with others — supporting brain health, easing loneliness, and building a genuine sense of belonging.

Meals prepared fresh in our full-size kitchen, shaped around each resident's preferences, needs, and appetite.

Mobility Support

Connection Is Care

Why social engagement matters for the brain.

Regular social interaction helps keep memory, attention, and communication active — while reducing the risk of depression and loneliness, both linked to broader health decline.

We weave connection into daily life through structured group activities — games, fitness classes, discussion groups, and creative workshops — plus communal dining that gives residents regular opportunities to build relationships, and outings and celebrations that nurture a real sense of community.

Choosing Care

Facility-based vs. home-style assisted living.

Both models provide support and safety, but the day-to-day experience feels very different. Here's the honest comparison.

Renewity (Home-Style) Facility-Based Community
Setting Warm, residential home Large, institutional or hotel-like
Residents A small, intimate group of 20 A high number of residents
Caregiver Ratio ~5:1 ~20:1
Time per Resident More time, personalized attention Limited, due to higher workload
Approach Relationship-centered, individualized Task-oriented, schedule-driven
Dining Home-cooked, family-style Large-scale, standardized menus
Feel Calm, familiar, a sense of belonging Efficient, but can feel impersonal

With a low caregiver-to-resident ratio, thoughtfully prepared meals, and daily opportunities for meaningful connection, Renewity is a place where residents are truly known — and families can feel confident their loved one is genuinely valued, just as they would be at home.
