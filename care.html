Care & Services

Everyday support, delivered like family would.

Renewity provides non-medical assistance with daily living in a home setting — help with the everyday, not intensive clinical treatment.

Personal Care

Bathing, dressing, grooming, and hygiene support — with patience, privacy, and respect for each resident's pace.

Memory Care

A calm, consistent routine for residents living with Alzheimer's or dementia, guided by caregivers trained in memory support.

Home-Cooked Nutrition

Meals prepared fresh in the home's own kitchen, shaped around each resident's preferences, needs, and appetite.

Daily Activities

Games, music, gardening, and conversation woven through the day — companionship, not just supervision.

Awake Overnight Care

A caregiver is present and awake throughout the night, every night — not on-call from somewhere else.

Housekeeping & Laundry

A clean, comfortable home maintained every day, so families never have to wonder or ask.

Medication Reminders

Support staying on schedule with medications, coordinated with each resident's own physician.

Family Communication

Proactive updates on how your loved one is doing — not silence until something's wrong.

Mobility Support

Help getting around the home safely, encouraging as much independence as each resident is able to keep.

The Question We'd Rather You Ask

"Is Renewity a nursing home?"

No — and the difference matters for your family's planning. Here's the honest comparison.

Renewity (Residential Care Home) A Skilled Nursing Facility
Setting A residential house in a neighborhood A clinical, hospital-like building
Purpose Help with daily living, long-term Intensive medical care & rehab, often short-term
Staffing Caregivers trained in personal & daily support Licensed nurses providing medical treatment
Feel A home, with a household rhythm A medical facility, with a clinical rhythm

If your loved one needs ongoing medical treatment or rehabilitation, a skilled nursing facility may be the right fit. If they need help with everyday living — while staying in a real home, with people who know them — that's exactly what Renewity was built for.
