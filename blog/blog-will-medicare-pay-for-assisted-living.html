Will Medicare Pay for Assisted Living in Indiana?

2026-11-15

This is one of the most common questions families ask when they start exploring senior care: does Medicare cover assisted living? The short answer is no — Medicare generally does not pay for the ongoing cost of assisted living or residential care homes. Understanding why can help you plan your next steps with less confusion.

What Medicare Typically Covers

Medicare is designed primarily around medical care, not long-term custodial care. It can help with things like:

  • Hospital stays
  • Doctor visits
  • Short-term skilled nursing care after a qualifying hospital stay
  • Certain medical equipment and therapies

What Medicare usually does not cover is the day-to-day support that assisted living and residential care homes provide — help with bathing, dressing, meals, medication reminders, and supervision. That kind of support is considered custodial care, not medical care, and it falls outside Medicare's scope.

Why This Surprises So Many Families

It's an easy mix-up. Many people assume that because Medicare covers skilled nursing facilities for a limited time after surgery or a hospital stay, it will also cover longer-term care in a residential setting. In reality, those are very different types of care with very different funding rules. Renewity is a congregate care home — not a skilled nursing facility — which is an important distinction to understand as you weigh your options.

So Where Do Families Look Instead?

Since Medicare isn't typically the answer, families usually explore a mix of other resources, such as:

  • Personal savings and retirement income
  • Long-term care insurance policies, if one was purchased in advance
  • Veterans benefits, for those who qualify
  • Medicaid programs, which have their own eligibility rules and are separate from Medicare

Every family's situation is different, and the right combination of resources depends on individual circumstances. We'd always encourage you to talk with a financial advisor or benefits counselor who specializes in senior care funding to get guidance specific to your situation.
