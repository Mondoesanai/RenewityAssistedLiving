Family FAQ

The questions every family asks.

Choosing care for someone you love is a big decision. Here are honest, direct answers to the questions families bring up most — no runaround, and no guessing on the things we're not sure about either.

Yes. Our caregivers help with the everyday personal tasks that come with aging — bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, and general hygiene — with patience and privacy. Home-cooked meals are prepared fresh around each resident's preferences, and caregivers help residents stay mobile and safe around the home. For medications, we provide reminders and coordinate with each resident's own physician — we are not licensed to administer medications or provide skilled nursing care.

Yes — residents living with Alzheimer's or other forms of dementia are part of our community. Our caregivers are trained in redirection and calm communication, residents follow a consistent daily routine, and our memory care areas are set up with resident safety and wandering risk in mind. We're glad to go over those details with your family in person, during a tour.

Yes — a caregiver is awake and present in the home throughout the night, every night. Renewity maintains a resident-to-caregiver ratio of about 5:1, compared to the roughly 20:1 ratio common at larger, facility-based communities.

In many cases, yes — we adjust each resident's care plan over time rather than asking them to move for every change in their needs. Every situation is different, though, so our caregiving team continually reassesses and will always be honest with your family about whether Renewity remains the right fit, or whether a higher level of care would serve them better.

Our rates are based on the level of personal care each resident needs, with any additional support — like extra personal care, medication assistance, or memory care — reflected in the monthly rate. We keep pricing straightforward with no hidden fees, and we're happy to go over current rates with your family directly.

Renewity operates as a residential care home, providing non-medical assistance with activities of daily living — we are not licensed or staffed to provide skilled nursing or medical treatment. Indiana senior living communities fall under oversight from the Family and Social Services Administration and the Indiana State Department of Health's Division of Long-Term Care, and families can review general licensing and inspection resources at in.gov/health/ltc. We're happy to answer any specific questions about our standing directly.
